## Verified organization identity

- Public name: Nova Royals AC.
- Tagline: “Building a Family of Sports Lovers.”
- Northern Virginia nonprofit 501(c)(3), described as helping youth and adults embrace active, healthy lives through soccer, cricket, and community.
- Website states the club has operated since 2022.
- Mission: create sports players, coaches, and teams of strong character while supporting physical, mental, and emotional growth.
- Stated values: Community, Growth, and Family.
- Listed location: Sully Highlands Park, Herndon, Virginia.
- Main contact: (703) 220-0763 and infonovaroyals@gmail.com.

- Fall Soccer Training 2026: 12 practice sessions; September 13–November 22, 2026; $120 per player; each additional sibling receives 50% off ($60).
- Women’s Soccer, all ages: competitive and recreational, welcoming all skill levels.
- Veterans Soccer, ages 35+: competition, camaraderie, and continued play.

- Recreational Soccer, listed as all ages: relaxed, fun-focused, community-driven play.

The current website Programs page does not list a distinct Men’s Soccer card, although men’s/adult soccer is a required ROYALS product audience from the product brief. Its app content remains clearly labeled as demo/assumption until the club supplies current details.

## Verified registration behavior

The Programs page links to one parameterized registration page with program values for Kids Soccer, Women’s Soccer, Veterans Soccer, Travel Soccer, Recreational Soccer, and CCPL Cricket.

Kids Soccer shows:

- program;
- guardian full name, email, and phone;
- street address, city, state, and ZIP;
- one to five children;
- first/last name, date of birth, and age per child;
- a live first-child/sibling total;
- required parent/guardian consent;
- typed parent/guardian signature;
- read-only signature date;
- continue-to-payment action.

The live consent text combines participation risk acknowledgment and authority for Nova Royals staff to seek emergency treatment. The app separates these into two explicit required acknowledgments for clarity while preserving the intent.

Adult Women’s Soccer shows player first/last name, email, phone, date of birth, address, player/parent signature, and signature date. It correctly omits the visible child count and guardian workflow. ROYALS follows the same program-aware principle with a shorter progressive adult form.

- Website body type is Open Sans; headings mix Montserrat and Playfair Display.
- Site variables use near-black `#1A1A1A`, charcoal `#2D2D2D`, warm-white `#FFFDF9`, cream `#FDF8F0`, and gold `#C9A227`.
- ROYALS deliberately evolves the system toward the requested kit-inspired orange (`#F36A21`) for actions, using deep charcoal and warm cream for continuity. It does not mechanically clone the website’s editorial serif treatment.

Remote website images are suitable for this internal demonstration. Public app builds should bundle optimized originals after Nova Royals confirms ownership/usage rights and supplies high-resolution source assets.
