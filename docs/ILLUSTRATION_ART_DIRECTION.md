# ROYALS illustration art direction

Temporary placeholders in the app are **not** production artwork. One-shot AI mascots and pennants must not ship as the club look.

## Process

1. Approve **one** base illustration (character or pennant) before any derivatives.
2. Prefer commissioning a Nepali or local DMV illustrator for the original ROYALS character / pennant.
3. AI may generate variations only from that approved source.
4. Club story: the illustration system was created by a local community artist.

Cursor may build the empty-state component architecture (`IllustrationFrame`) and keep placeholders labeled **TEMPORARY ART**.

## Base style (to approve before volume)

- **Line:** confident ink line, slightly imperfect, not hairline vector noise.
- **Shapes:** simple geometric fields, pennants, balls; avoid hyper-detailed stadiums.
- **Texture:** paper grain or light hatch, cream ground (`#F6EFE4` family).
- **Palette:** cream, black, ROYALS orange `#F36A21`, restrained charcoal. No neon gradients.
- **Character proportions:** friendly, athletic, not baby-cute chibi and not hyper-real.
- **Equipment:** soccer, cricket, and training kit that a Northern Virginia club would actually use. No generic European mega-club kits.
- **Expressions:** clear, calm, proud — not smirking stock “AI mascot.”

## Prohibit

- Plastic skin, extra fingers, melted logos
- Uncanny cubs, random crowns, clip-art trophies
- Watermarks, stock-site palettes, generic “startup mascot” poses
- Treating the first generated image as final

## In product

High-frequency screens may use approved art later. About, sponsors, settings, and waiver history stay conventional and do not need custom illustration.
