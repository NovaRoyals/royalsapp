# Product assumptions

Verified organization facts used in this build:

- Organization: Nova Royals Athletic Club / Nova Royals AC.
- Identity language: “Building a Family of Sports Lovers.”
- The club serves youth and adults through soccer, cricket, fitness, and community activities.
- Fall Soccer Training 2026: ages 3–16; September 13 through November 22, 2026; 12 sessions; $120 first child; $60 each additional sibling.
- The current registration asks for program/player/guardian/contact/address information, per-child profiles, parent consent, emergency-treatment consent, typed signatures, and date.
- The organization references CCPL cricket participation and adult external-league participation.
- The live Programs page lists Kids Soccer, Women’s Soccer, Veterans Soccer, Travel Soccer, Recreational Soccer, and CCPL. It describes CCPL as the Capital Cricket Premier League.
- The website states the club has operated since 2022 and lists Sully Highlands Park in Herndon, Virginia.
- The concluded 1st Royals Cup ran April 4–5, 2026 in Chantilly for 35+ veterans, in an 8-a-side format with a $550 team entry.
- The fourth Royals Run was listed for August 15, 2026 at Burke Lake Park.

Assumptions made for product demonstration:

- Sample women’s and cricket player names, scores, standings, and some club events remain invented demo data unless labeled otherwise.
- Men’s Open 8v8 Fall 2026 fixtures, kickoff times, fields, home/visitor designation, and the Sunday-evening registered squad were provided by the club and are treated as current club data. Results and league standings were not supplied and are not invented.
- Nottoway Park is labeled Vienna, VA and EC Lawrence Park is labeled Chantilly, VA from common Fairfax County venue names; street addresses were not supplied.
- Opponent names are stored as received (including “rundown fc” and “Ref Do Something FC”).
- “Royals Autumn Cup,” its October dates, format, fee, eligibility, and deadline are an invented tournament concept.
- Specific women’s, men’s, veterans, recreational, travel, and cricket registration status/pricing are placeholders unless independently confirmed.
- Northern Virginia/Fairfax location labels are intentionally broad where the source does not provide an app-ready venue.
- Program photography uses organization-hosted website images. Bundle optimized source originals only after the club confirms app usage rights.
- The geometric “R” mark is an in-app placeholder brand abstraction, not a replacement for the legal Nova Royals crest.
- The app starts in a seeded parent state so leadership can immediately evaluate the highest-priority experience. Profile role controls expose guest/player/coach/volunteer/admin states. First-run onboarding can still be replayed; “Preview as parent” keeps the board path.
- Fitness is a program vertical (not a tab). Weather, payments, and calendar writes remain stubs.
- Demo “payment” records remain pending or locally marked paid and never charge a card.
- Consent wording in the MVP is concise placeholder language based on the source form’s intent. It requires legal/club approval and versioning before accepting real registrations.
- Age is not recomputed into public display. Production code should derive eligibility server-side on a specified season cutoff date.
- External competition data will initially be maintained/imported by authorized club staff and labeled with its source; no official FXA/CCPL API availability is assumed.
- Brackets are represented as configuration plus seeded UI architecture; automatic progression is a later feature.

Open organizational inputs that do not block this MVP:

- Approved high-resolution logo, icon, kit photography, and image usage rights.
- Final brand typeface licenses and exact color standards.
- Program capacities, field assignments, cancellation policy, refund policy, and included equipment.
- Production waiver language and records retention policy.
- Payment provider, merchant account, fee policy, and tax/receipt requirements.
- Exact staff roster and role assignments.
- External league data permissions/import method.
- Push notification credentials, app-store identifiers, and deep-link domains.
